Myiva Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026

1. Introduction

Myiva Pro helps you manage your Bookkeeping (Myiva) compliance requirements. This policy briefly details how we handle your personal data and keep it safe.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect and store the basic information needed to run your account:

  • Identity Details: Your name and email address.
  • Uploaded Media: Photos, documents, receipts, and files you explicitly add to the system.

3. Sign Up & Account Creation

We offer a straightforward sign-up process. When registering an account, your registration credentials are encrypted and stored safely to grant you private access.

4. Your Control & Deletion Rights

You have full ownership of your data profile. We allow you to completely delete your account and erase your stored metrics at any time.

You can do this instantly by visiting: More → Profile → Delete Account inside the App.

5. Data Sharing

We never sell your files, images, or email credentials to data brokers or advertisers.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about your personal records, contact us at: info@corientbs.co.uk
